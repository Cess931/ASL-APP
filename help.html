Getting Started

Quick-start answers and an overview to help new users get familiar with the platform.

What is this App?

Learn what the ASL site offers, including games, practice tools, and memory cards to help learn sign language.

How to use the App?

Stand 1–2 feet away from the camera to capture a half‑body, then reenact the displayed sign language.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a Practice? +
To start a Practice, go to the 'Practice' page, select a mode, and press Play. You can choose difficulty to match your level.
Can I study offline? +
No. The site does not currently have a fully offline mode.
