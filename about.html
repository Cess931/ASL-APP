Our Mission

We are dedicated to making American Sign Language accessible to everyone. Our app provides an easy and interactive way to learn ASL at your own pace, helping bridge communication gaps and foster true inclusivity. This project primarily aligns with SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities, emphasizing the importance of promoting universal, social, economic, and political inclusion for all by 2030.
